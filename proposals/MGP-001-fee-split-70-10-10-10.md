## Summary

Send 70% of every loan fee to $MAGPIE holders, 10% to SOL LPs, 10% to referrers, and 10% to the protocol reserve. Replaces the current 10% holder share with a holder-first split. Forward-only — distributions already accrued under the old split are not retroactively re-cut.

## The new split

| Recipient | Share | BPS |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| $MAGPIE holders | 70% | 7,000 |
| SOL LPs | 10% | 1,000 |
| Referrers | 10% | 1,000 |
| Protocol reserve | 10% | 1,000 |
| **Total** | **100%** | **10,000** |

## Why this proposal

The current 80/10/5/2/3 split allocates 80% of loan fees to SOL LPs and only 10% to $MAGPIE holders. The protocol launched with that split as bootstrap economics — LPs needed the bulk to seed the lending pool before fees compounded. Six weeks in, with the pool consistently producing fee flow and $MAGPIE holders becoming the protocol's economic backbone, the split should reflect that.

70/10/10/10 puts holders first, keeps LPs whole at a still-meaningful share, and creates an explicit protocol reserve slice for the first time. Referrer share moves up to 10% to match the new emphasis on growth-via-existing-users.
